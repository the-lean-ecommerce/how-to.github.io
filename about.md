# About

The Lean Ecommerce How-To is a practical guide library for operators and builders who want clearer technical systems.

This blog is focused on tutorials: the specific steps, tradeoffs, commands, workflows, and product decisions that help ecommerce teams get useful work shipped. The topics sit where ecommerce, SaaS, automation, AI, and product operations overlap.

The mission is to make technical execution feel calmer. A good how-to should reduce ambiguity, explain the why behind the steps, and help a lean team understand what to do next without wading through vague advice.

Expect guides about:

- Ecommerce operations, storefront workflows, and marketplace tooling.
- SaaS products that help small teams automate or simplify work.
- AI-assisted content, image, video, analytics, and support systems.
- Developer-friendly setup notes for publishing, APIs, integrations, and static sites.
- Practical product decisions for founders, marketers, and operators.

The broader project lives at [The Lean Ecommerce](https://the-lean-ecommerce.com).
